rush on income. Every plan is broken into chunks that fit one evening.

Maintenance retainers are already live on `/services` (Basic $99, Plus $199) —

## Suggested order

They share material, so do them in sequence rather than in parallel:

1. **Writing, starting now, ongoing.** One post every two weeks. Costs little,
   and it shows which topics people care about before you build anything bigger.
2. **Starter kit** in the off-weeks between posts. It's mostly packaging code
   that already exists in this repo.
3. **Course** once 2–3 posts on the Docker / reverse-proxy topic have real
   traffic. The kit becomes the course's companion code.
4. **Micro-SaaS** last. It's the biggest build and the kit is its foundation.
   Do the vertical validation (phase 0) early though, since it's just talking
   to people and can run alongside everything else.

Rule of thumb: never have more than one *build* in progress at a time. Writing
is the only thing that runs alongside.
